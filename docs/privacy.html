Privacy Policy — ShortClip Mac

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Local-First Privacy

ShortClip Mac runs locally on your macOS device. We do not operate external backend servers, analytics trackers, or third-party data collection pipelines.

2. Information Accessed via Official TikTok APIs

  • Profile Information (user.info.basic): Used solely to display your TikTok display name, avatar, and profile statistics inside the app on your Mac.
  • Public Video List (video.list): Used solely to display your own public videos inside the app's Profile and Feed views.

3. Credentials & Storage

  • The Application never collects, sees, or stores your TikTok password.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally in your macOS Keychain.
  • Web session cookies are managed directly by Apple's WKWebsiteDataStore on your device.

4. Data Sharing & Deletion

We do not sell or share user data with third parties. You can clear all local tokens and cookies anytime from Settings inside the application.
